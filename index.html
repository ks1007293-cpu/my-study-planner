Frequently asked questions

Find answers to the most common questions about using MyStudyLife.

What is MyStudyLife?

MyStudyLife is a smart planner made for students. It helps you organize your classes, tasks, exams, and extracurriculars all in one place – and syncs across all your devices so you’re always up to date.

Can I use MyStudyLife for rotating timetables?

Yes! MyStudyLife supports rotating timetables, including custom class schedules that shift daily or weekly. Whether it’s Day A/B, Week 1/2, or something unique to your school – you can stay organized without needing a separate calendar.

Will I get reminders?

Absolutely. MyStudyLife sends smart reminders for your upcoming tasks, classes, and exams – including alerts for overdue homework – so you never miss a deadline.

Can I access MyStudyLife on all my devices?

Yes – your schedule and tasks sync automatically across web, iOS, and Android. That means you can plan on your laptop and check it later on your phone without missing a beat.

Is there a version for parents?

Yes! Family Connect is our companion app that helps parents stay in the loop with their child’s classes, assignments, and exam schedule – without needing to constantly ask. It’s designed to support student success without the stress.
